# Selection

Depth beats filling slots. Prefer maintainer follow-ups on merged work, endorsed unclaimed issues, then unchecked tracking goals. Each unit needs an objective done-check and a user-visible benefit. TypeScript/React/Node tooling and frontend/full-stack work fit best; other languages are fine when valuable and locally verifiable.

Start from a concrete unresolved issue and reuse the repository record in `ledger/repo-records/` while current. Reject a repository for unresponsiveness only on the long record in the selection policy, never on a short window, a small sample, stars or a flood index. There is no cooldown or count cap.

Look for invariant breaks from supported real usage; prefer conformance/parity goals, real performance gains and code where accepted work gives context. A report's age is no reason to skip it, but check fresh ones for an open or linked PR, claim or assignee before starting and before ship. For closed attempts, read why they closed.

A long-shipped behavior with a documented workaround may be deliberate. Propose a design question rather than labeling it a regression. Do not add docs/typo/cosmetic padding or target a line count. Zero worthwhile candidates is a successful scout.
