How this factory works

  1. Find demand

    Look for unclaimed problems in projects that welcome automated contributions.

  2. Prove the change

    Qualify the value, make the smallest useful repair and capture executable evidence.

  3. Review independently

    Separate implementation, independent review and coordinator release checks.

  4. Ship and learn

    Disclose automation, follow feedback and use acceptance and quality to improve the factory.
